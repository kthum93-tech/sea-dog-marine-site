How to Make Your Dock Ladder Safe for Kids and Guests

By Sea Dog Marine · Serving Pinellas, Hillsborough and Manatee Counties · 2 min read

For a lot of waterfront families, the dock ladder gets used more than anything else on the property. Kids swim off it, guests climb out of paddleboards on it and everyone uses it to get on and off the boat. When it is covered in barnacles and slime, it becomes a hazard.

The problem with growth on ladders

  • Barnacle shells are sharp enough to cut hands and feet.
  • Slimy rungs cause slips, especially for kids.
  • Growth hides loose mounts and corroded fasteners.

Quick safety habits

  • Check the ladder before every swim season and after storms.
  • Wear water shoes when using ladders and swimming near pilings.
  • Make sure the ladder is firmly mounted and does not wobble.
  • Keep a first aid kit near the dock for small cuts.

Professional ladder degrowth

We remove barnacles, oysters, algae and slime from aluminum, stainless and wood ladders, including rungs, rails and mounting brackets. We also check the mounts and let you know if anything looks loose.

Pair it with your dock cleaning

Ladder cleaning takes little time and is easy to add to a dock, seawall or lift cleaning. Many families schedule it right before summer so the swim season starts clean.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can barnacles on a dock ladder cause injuries?

Yes. Barnacle shells are sharp and can cut hands and feet, and slimy rungs cause slips.

When should I clean my dock ladder?

Before swim season and after storms, or anytime growth or slime builds up.
