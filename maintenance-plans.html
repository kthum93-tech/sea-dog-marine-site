Waterfront Maintenance Plans and Storm Prep

The easiest way to keep a waterfront property looking great is to never let growth get out of hand. Sea Dog Marine maintenance plans put your dock, seawall, pilings, ladders and lift on a regular schedule, with storm prep and post storm cleanup built in.

What is included

  • Scheduled cleanings on a set interval
  • Pre hurricane season inspection and cleaning
  • Post storm debris and growth cleanup
  • Photo report after every visit
  • Priority scheduling for plan members
  • Great for rentals, HOAs and part time owners

Why it matters

Regular service costs less than heavy cleanups and catches damage early. For seasonal residents and rental owners, it means coming back to a clean, safe dock every time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a waterfront maintenance plan include?

Plans are built around your property and can include dock, seawall, piling, ladder and boat lift cleaning on a set schedule, plus storm prep and photo reports.

Do you work with HOAs and property managers?

Yes. We service community docks, marinas and multi home seawalls across Pinellas, Hillsborough and Manatee counties.

What is included in storm prep?

We clean and inspect your dock, pilings, lift and seawall before storm season and document their condition with photos, which can help with insurance claims later.
