Dock, Seawall and Piling Cleaning in Pinellas County, FL

Pinellas County has more private docks and seawalls per mile of shoreline than almost anywhere in Florida. Between the Intracoastal, Boca Ciega Bay, Clearwater Harbor and Old Tampa Bay, warm salt water feeds fast barnacle and oyster growth on every piling, ladder and lift.

Canal homes in Snell Isle, Shore Acres, Isla del Sol, Island Estates and the beach towns see heavy growth on the waterline band of seawall caps and pilings. We clean from the water and the dock so your yard and landscaping stay untouched.
