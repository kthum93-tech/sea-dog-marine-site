Tampa Bay Service Areas

Sea Dog Marine provides dock, seawall, piling, ladder and boat lift cleaning for waterfront properties across Pinellas, Hillsborough and Manatee counties. Pick a county below for the cities we serve and the same marine cleaning services.

Pinellas County

Pinellas County has more private docks and seawalls per mile of shoreline than almost anywhere in Florida. Between the Intracoastal, Boca Ciega Bay, Clearwater Harbor and Old Tampa Bay, warm salt water feeds fast barnacle and oyster growth on every piling, ladder and lift.

Canal homes in Snell Isle, Shore Acres, Isla del Sol, Island Estates and the beach towns see heavy growth on the waterline band of seawall caps and pilings. We clean from the water and the dock so your yard and landscaping stay untouched.

Hillsborough County

From the canals of Apollo Beach to the bayfront homes of South Tampa and Davis Islands, Hillsborough County waterfront owners deal with brackish and salt water growth that shortens the life of docks, lifts and seawalls.

Apollo Beach and Ruskin canal communities see heavy oyster build up on seawalls and lift cradles. Hillsborough River and bay properties fight algae, slime and barnacles that make ladders and walkways dangerous.

Dock, seawall and piling cleaning in Hillsborough County. Cities and communities we serve there:

Manatee County

Manatee County waterfront, from the Manatee River to Anna Maria Island and Longboat Key, combines strong tidal flow with warm Gulf water. That mix carries constant barnacle, oyster and algae growth onto docks, pilings and boat lifts.

Island homes on Anna Maria and Holmes Beach and river homes in Bradenton and Palmetto all benefit from a regular cleaning schedule, especially before and after storm season.
