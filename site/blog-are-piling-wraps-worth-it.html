Are Piling Wraps Worth It for Tampa Bay Docks?

Quick answer. A piling wrap is a barrier around a wood piling in the tidal zone that blocks marine borers, slows rot and keeps barnacles and oysters off the wood. Wraps belong on pilings that are still sound, and they have to go on clean wood. A badly damaged piling may need repair or replacement first.

Wood pilings hold up your dock and boat lift, and the tidal zone is where they take the most abuse. Piling wraps are one of the most common upgrades Tampa Bay homeowners ask us about. Here is how to decide if they make sense for you.

What a piling wrap does

A piling wrap is a protective barrier installed around a wood piling through the tidal zone. It shields the wood from marine borers, slows rot and keeps barnacles and oysters from attaching directly to the wood.

Why pilings fail in the tidal zone

The section of a piling between low and high tide is wet, then dry, then wet again every day. Marine borers and fungi thrive there. Once the wood thins out, the piling loses strength, and replacing it means work from the water and a bigger bill.

When wraps make the most sense

  • On pilings that are still sound but starting to show wear.
  • On newer docks, to protect the investment from day one.
  • On boat lift pilings that carry heavy loads.
  • When you plan to keep the home for years and want the dock to last.

When wraps may not be enough

If a piling is already badly damaged, wrapping it will not restore strength. In that case it may need repair or replacement first. We check each piling during cleaning and let you know which are good candidates.

Why cleaning comes first

Wraps need to go on clean wood. We remove all barnacles, oysters and growth before installing so the wrap fits tight and does its job.

The bottom line

For most waterfront homes, cleaning and wrapping sound pilings is a small cost compared with replacing them. Text us a video of your dock and we will tell you which pilings would benefit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do piling wraps protect against?

Wraps help protect wood pilings from marine borers, rot and direct marine growth in the tidal zone.

Can damaged pilings be wrapped?

Sound pilings are ideal. Heavily damaged pilings may need repair or replacement before wrapping.
