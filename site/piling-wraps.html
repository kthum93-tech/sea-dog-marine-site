Piling Wraps and Dock Protection

Piling wraps seal wood pilings in the tidal zone, where marine borers, rot and growth do the most damage. Sea Dog Marine cleans each piling first, then installs protective wraps that help your dock last years longer.

What is included

  • Full cleaning before wrapping
  • Protective wrap installation through the tidal zone
  • Wraps for dock and boat lift pilings
  • Neat, secure, finished look
  • Recommendations on which pilings need it most

Why it matters

Replacing a piling is expensive and disruptive. Wrapping is a fraction of that cost and is one of the best investments a waterfront owner can make in dock longevity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do piling wraps really work?

Wraps create a barrier that blocks marine borers and slows rot in the tidal zone, which is where wood pilings usually fail first.

Can you wrap pilings that already have damage?

We will assess each piling during the inspection. Sound pilings are great candidates. Pilings with heavy damage may need repair or replacement first.
