Propeller and Running Gear Cleaning in Tampa Bay

Even a thin layer of barnacles on a propeller can cost you speed and add vibration. Sea Dog Marine cleans props, shafts, struts and rudders in the water so your boat runs the way it should.

What it is

Propeller cleaning is a diver removing barnacles and other hard growth from the blades. The same dive usually covers the shaft, strut and rudder. The boat stays in the water at your dock, lift or slip. This is cleaning, not propeller repair or balancing.

Why it matters in Tampa Bay saltwater

A propeller is a small surface that the engine depends on. In Tampa Bay salt water, barnacles settle on blades that sit still, on canal lifts in Hillsborough as much as on bay docks in Pinellas and Manatee. Even a light crust changes how the prop bites the water. Owners usually notice it as lost speed or a vibration that was not there before. Cleaning the prop is separate from cleaning the whole hull, and most boats have both done on one dive.

The process

  1. Text a photo of the boat and where it is kept to 727-699-4920, along with the boat length.
  2. The diver cleans the blades, then the shaft, strut and rudder.
  3. Hard growth comes off. The diver looks for obvious dings or a bent blade and tells you what is visible. Bent blades need a prop shop, which is a different job.
  4. Shaft and prop zincs are checked. Replacement is available on the same visit if they are worn.
  5. The propeller can be cleaned on the same dive as the hull if you want both done.

What is included

  • Propeller blade cleaning
  • Shaft, strut and rudder cleaning
  • Barnacle and hard growth removal
  • Visual check for dings or damage
  • Zinc check on the shaft and prop

Props are precision parts. Growth on the blades hurts performance and fuel economy, and cleaning them is one of the fastest ways to get a sluggish boat running right again.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my boat feel slow or vibrate?

Barnacles and growth on the propeller and running gear are a common cause. Cleaning the prop often brings back lost speed and smooths out vibration.

Can prop cleaning be added to a hull cleaning?

Yes. Most customers have the prop and running gear cleaned on the same dive as the hull and boat bottom cleaning.

Do you repair bent propellers?

No. We clean growth off the prop and tell you if a blade looks dinged or bent. Straightening, welding and balancing are a prop shop job, not an in-water cleaning.
