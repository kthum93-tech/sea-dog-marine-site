Free Video Inspection and Quote

No waiting around for a site visit. Text us a short video and we will tell you exactly what your dock, seawall, pilings or lift need and what it will cost.

What you get

Text a short video or photos of your dock, seawall or lift to 727-699-4920. We review it and send back a recommendation and a price, often the same day, with no site visit needed to get started. The recommendation says what the dock, seawall, pilings or lift needs. Price depends on size, amount of growth and access.

How it works

  1. Send a video. Text a short video or photos to 727-699-4920. A 30 to 60 second video walking the dock or seawall is enough, with close ups of pilings, ladders and lift cradles. Include your address or nearest city, your name and the best time to reach you.
  2. Get a price. We review it and send a clear recommendation and quote, usually the same day.
  3. We clean and restore. The crew removes algae, barnacles, oysters, mold and marine growth, then documents the work with before and after photos.
  4. We protect it. Add piling wraps or a maintenance plan to keep growth from coming back. Plans can include scheduled cleanings, storm prep and a photo report after every visit.

What to send

  • A 30 to 60 second video walking the dock or seawall
  • Close ups of pilings, ladders and lift cradles
  • Your address or nearest city
  • Your name and the best time to reach you
