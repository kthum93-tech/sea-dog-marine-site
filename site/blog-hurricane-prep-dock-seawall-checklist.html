Hurricane Season Dock and Seawall Prep Checklist for Tampa Bay

By Sea Dog Marine · Serving Pinellas, Hillsborough and Manatee Counties · 2 min read

Tampa Bay waterfront homes have learned the hard way how much damage storm surge and wind can do to docks, lifts and seawalls. Good prep before the season starts protects your property and gives you a clear record of its condition for insurance.

Before the season starts

  • Schedule a cleaning and inspection of your dock, pilings, seawall and lift in May or June.
  • Take dated photos and video of every part of your waterfront. A clean structure photographs far better than one covered in growth.
  • Note loose boards, missing fasteners, soft pilings and cracks and get them fixed early.
  • Check boat lift cables, bunks and hardware for wear.

When a storm is in the forecast

  • Remove or secure anything loose on the dock: furniture, hoses, kayaks, dock boxes.
  • Follow your boat lift manufacturer guidance for storm position and secure the boat or relocate it if needed.
  • Turn off power to the dock and lift at the breaker.
  • Tie down or remove ladders and removable steps if your design allows.

Why documentation matters

After a storm, insurers and contractors want to know what condition your dock and seawall were in before. Photos taken right after a professional cleaning show the true condition of pilings, boards and the wall face, without months of growth hiding the details.

Schedule cleaning and inspection in May or June, before peak hurricane season in Tampa Bay.
