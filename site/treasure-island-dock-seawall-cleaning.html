Dock, Seawall and Hull Cleaning in Treasure Island, FL

Treasure Island is a barrier island with the Gulf on the west and Boca Ciega Bay and the Intracoastal on the east. Most private docks and seawalls are on the bay side, where calmer salt water grows barnacles and oysters on pilings and caps.

Bay-side lifts and ladders pick up shell because the water is warm and sheltered. John's Pass, the inlet at the south end, sits between Treasure Island and Madeira Beach. Cleaning usually covers the dock, seawall, pilings and ladder. A diver can clean the hull and propeller and check zincs while the boat is at the dock.

Call or text 727-699-4920 and we will look at a short video of the dock, seawall or boat. You can also send a free video inspection.

Which side of Treasure Island do you clean?

Do you clean ladders and lifts on Treasure Island?
