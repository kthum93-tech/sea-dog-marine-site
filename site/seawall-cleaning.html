Seawall Cleaning in Pinellas, Hillsborough and Manatee Counties

Your seawall protects your yard, your pool and your home. When it is covered in barnacles, oysters and algae, you cannot see the cracks, spalling or seepage that lead to costly failures. Sea Dog Marine strips marine growth from the wall face and cap so the wall looks right and you can see its true condition.

What is included

  • Seawall face cleaning down to the low tide line
  • Seawall cap cleaning
  • Barnacle and oyster removal
  • Algae, slime and stain removal
  • Visual condition check during cleaning
  • Photo and video documentation

Why it matters

Heavy oyster and barnacle growth hides cracks and joint failures. Cleaning the wall gives you, your insurer and your contractor a clear look at its condition before a small repair turns into a full replacement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is seawall cleaning worth it?

Yes. A clean wall looks better, adds curb appeal to waterfront homes and, more importantly, lets you catch cracks and seepage early when repairs cost far less.

Can you clean a seawall below the water?

We clean as far down the face as conditions allow and time our work with the tides to reach the band where most growth attaches.

Do you repair seawalls?

We document what we see during cleaning and let you know if anything looks like it needs attention, so you can plan repairs with confidence.
