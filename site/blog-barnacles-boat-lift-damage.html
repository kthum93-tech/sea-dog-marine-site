How Barnacles Damage Your Boat Lift and Your Boat

By Sea Dog Marine · Serving Pinellas, Hillsborough and Manatee Counties · 2 min read

A boat lift is supposed to protect your boat. When it is covered in barnacles and oysters, it can do the opposite. Tampa Bay water grows marine life fast, and lifts that sit lowered in the water collect it on every surface.

Extra weight and strain

Heavy growth on cradles, beams and pilings adds weight that your motor, gearbox and cables have to lift every time. Over months, that extra load adds wear.

Scratched hulls and torn bunks

Barnacles on bunks and guide posts scrape gelcoat and tear bunk carpet. Once bunk carpet is damaged, it holds even more growth.

Hidden corrosion

Growth traps salt water against metal parts. Bolts, brackets and beams can corrode underneath without being visible.

Signs your lift needs cleaning

  • Shells visible on cradles or bunks when the lift is raised.
  • The motor sounds strained or the lift moves slower than usual.
  • New scratches on your hull after lifting.
  • Guide posts covered in growth.

Keep your lift lowered less

When possible, keep the lift raised when not in use. Cradles that sit in the water grow much faster.

A simple maintenance rhythm

Frequently Asked Questions

Can barnacles damage my boat?

Yes. Barnacles on bunks and guide posts can scratch gelcoat and tear bunk carpet.

How often should a boat lift be cleaned?

Most Tampa Bay lifts need cleaning every 6 to 12 months, more often if cradles sit in the water.
