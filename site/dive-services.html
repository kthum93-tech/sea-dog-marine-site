Boat Dive Services in Tampa Bay

Sea Dog Marine does more than docks and seawalls. Our diver works on your boat in the water at your dock, lift or marina slip across Pinellas, Hillsborough and Manatee counties, so you can skip the haul out for routine underwater care.

Our dive services

  • Hull and Boat Bottom Cleaning: A diver cleans barnacles, slime and growth off your hull and running gear at your dock or slip.
  • Zinc Anode Replacement: A diver replaces worn zinc anodes on shafts, props and trim tabs without a haul out.
  • Propeller Cleaning: A diver removes barnacles and growth from props, shafts and struts so the boat runs smooth.

Why in-water service

Growth on a hull and running gear costs speed and fuel, and worn zinc anodes leave props, shafts and outdrives open to corrosion. Handling this in the water at your dock keeps the boat ready to use and avoids the cost and downtime of hauling it out for routine work.
