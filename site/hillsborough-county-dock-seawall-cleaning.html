Dock, Seawall and Piling Cleaning in Hillsborough County, FL

From the canals of Apollo Beach to the bayfront homes of South Tampa and Davis Islands, Hillsborough County waterfront owners deal with brackish and salt water growth that shortens the life of docks, lifts and seawalls.

Apollo Beach and Ruskin canal communities see heavy oyster build up on seawalls and lift cradles. Hillsborough River and bay properties fight algae, slime and barnacles that make ladders and walkways dangerous.
