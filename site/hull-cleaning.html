Hull and Boat Bottom Cleaning in Tampa Bay

Barnacles, grass and slime on a boat bottom slow you down, burn more fuel and make the engine work harder. Sea Dog Marine sends a diver to your dock or slip to clean the hull in the water, so your boat stays put and you do not pay for a haul out just to clean the bottom.

What it is

Hull and boat bottom cleaning is in-water work. A diver removes barnacles, slime and grass from the bottom while the boat stays at your dock, lift or slip. It is routine underwater care. It is not a haul out, and it is not a bottom paint job.

Why it matters in Tampa Bay saltwater

Tampa Bay stays warm most of the year, and the salt water in Pinellas, Hillsborough and Manatee counties grows barnacles and algae on a hull that sits still. Boats kept in canals and harbors often grow faster than boats that run often. Wiping growth off on a regular visit keeps hard shell from building into a layer that has to wait for a haul out. A cleaner bottom means less drag, so the boat moves more easily and the engine works less hard.

The process

  1. Text 727-699-4920 with the boat length, a photo of the boat, and the dock, lift or slip where it sits.
  2. We confirm the bottom can be cleaned in the water and send a price. The price depends on length and how much growth is on it.
  3. A diver cleans the bottom, running gear, strainers and trim tabs at the boat.
  4. Zinc anodes are checked on the same dive. Worn zincs can be replaced on that visit if you want that done.
  5. You get notes on anything that looks damaged. Photos or video are available if you ask for them.

What is included

  • Hull bottom cleaning in the water
  • Barnacle, slime and grass removal
  • Running gear, strainers and trim tabs
  • Check of zinc anodes while we are down there
  • Notes on anything that looks damaged
  • Photos or video on request

A clean bottom means better speed, better fuel economy and less strain on the engine. Regular cleaning also keeps hard growth from building up to the point where it takes a haul out to remove.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to haul my boat out for bottom cleaning?

No. A diver cleans the hull in the water at your dock, lift or slip, so there is no haul out needed just to clean the bottom.

How often should a boat bottom be cleaned in Tampa Bay?

It depends on your bottom paint, how often you run the boat and how fast growth comes back where it is kept. Text a photo or video and we will recommend a schedule for your boat.

Can you check my zincs at the same time?

Yes. We look at your zinc anodes during every hull cleaning and let you know when they need replacing. Zinc anode replacement can be added on the same dive.

Will hull cleaning strip my bottom paint?

The job is to remove growth, not to sand the paint off. Soft growth comes off with less work than hard barnacles. If the paint is already failing, we will tell you what we see. We do not repaint bottoms in the water.
