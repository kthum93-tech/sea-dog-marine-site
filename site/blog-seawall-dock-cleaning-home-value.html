Selling a Waterfront Home? Clean the Dock and Seawall First

Quick answer. A clean dock and seawall photograph better and let an inspector see the real condition instead of guessing under marine growth. Clean the boards, seawall face and cap, pilings, boat lift and ladders about one to two weeks before listing photos. That leaves time to fix small issues the cleaning reveals.

By Shea [TODO last name], Owner, Sea Dog Marine · Published: TODO · Updated: TODO · Serving Pinellas, Hillsborough and Manatee Counties · 2 min read

On a waterfront listing, the dock and seawall are part of what buyers are paying for. When they look neglected, buyers start wondering what else has been ignored. A professional cleaning before you list is one of the simplest ways to protect your price.

First impressions from the water side

Listing photos and drone shots almost always show the backyard and dock. Green boards, stained seawalls and crusty pilings stand out in those images.

Fewer surprises during inspection

Buyers often bring in inspectors who look closely at the seawall and dock. When the structures are clean, the inspector sees the real condition instead of guessing at what is under the growth. That leads to clearer reports and fewer last minute repair requests.

What to clean before listing

  • Dock boards, walkways and stairs.
  • Seawall face and cap.
  • Pilings through the tidal zone.
  • Boat lift, cradles and bunks.
  • Dock ladders.

Timing

Schedule cleaning one to two weeks before listing photos. That gives time to address any small repairs the cleaning reveals.
